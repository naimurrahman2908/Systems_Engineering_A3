\begin{abstract}
This paper describes the design and implementation of a low-cost line-following and obstacle-avoidance robot built using an Arduino Uno. The robot uses two IR sensors to follow a black line, two HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensors to detect obstacles, and an L293D motor driver to control two DC motors. Before building the prototype, a systems engineering approach was used to model the robot with SysML diagrams (block definition, internal block, activity, sequence, use case, and state machine diagrams) to better understand the system's structure and behaviour. The robot was designed to follow a track, avoid the first obstacle it detects, and perform a 180° turn upon detecting a second obstacle, before continuing back along the line in the opposite direction. The completed prototype was tested on a custom track with curves and obstacles, and it completed 10 out of 10 trials successfully, giving a 100% success rate. These results show that simple IR and ultrasonic sensing combined with an Arduino-based control system can achieve reliable line-following and obstacle-avoidance behaviour.
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract}
This paper presents a low-cost line-following and obstacle-avoidance robot built on an Arduino Uno. Two IR sensors enable line tracking, while two HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensors handle obstacle detection; an L293D motor driver controls the two DC motors. Prior to construction, a systems engineering approach using SysML diagrams (block definition, internal block, activity, sequence, use case, and state machine) modeled the robot's structure and behavior. The design follows a track, avoids the first detected obstacle, then performs a 180° turn at a second obstacle before retracing the line in reverse. Testing on a custom track with curves and obstacles yielded a 100 percent success rate across 10 trials, confirming reliable performance.
\end{abstract}
